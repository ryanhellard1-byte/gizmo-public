# MENDS-X NASA Orbital Clarity Phase-1 Go/No-Go

Current official dates verified 2026-09-10:
- Registration deadline: **2026-10-28, 5:00 PM ET**
- Submission deadline: **2026-11-11, 5:00 PM ET**
- Up to four Phase-1 winners: **$200,000 each**

NASA Phase-1 scoring uses four criteria on a 1–5 scale with at least five judges per eligible submission and normalized scoring.

## Relevance — target 5/5
GO only if:
- direct neutral mass density is clearly primary;
- altitude regime/range is explicit;
- composition is secondary and quality-gated;
- commercial hosted-payload proliferation is central to the value proposition.

## Performance — target 4/5 now, 5/5 if physical evidence closes
GO only if:
- all computational claims are traceable to retained inputs/outputs/version/hash;
- E.6 14/18/22/35/100 eV + 20/80 V/m architecture is used consistently;
- modeled results are labeled modeled, not demonstrated;
- uncertainty is tied to P95/statistical meaning rather than mixing 1-sigma RSS with top-level limits;
- stale 20/60/70/85 and incompatible geometry language is removed from final materials;
- rank/condition-number claims are omitted unless exact matrix/SVD provenance is archived;
- final altitude claim is no broader than evidence supports.

Score mover if available before deadline: at least one credible G0–G4 breadboard dataset, especially measured response/EEDF plus blind mixtures.

## Cost & scalability — target 5/5 only with quote support
GO only if:
- recurring cost excludes NRE, launch, deployment, and operations;
- 100-unit delivered-cost model includes labor, calibration, acceptance, yield/rework, screening, and overhead;
- dated supplier/facility quotations support key line items;
- production method and approximate production rate are quantified;
- any $99M–$999M comparison is explicitly labeled a potential 100-unit acquisition-cost scenario, not guaranteed NASA savings.

## Flight readiness — target 4–5/5
GO only if:
- mass, volume, average/peak power, data rate/format, pointing, mission life, thermal, radiation, vibration/shock, EMI/EMC, contamination, and safety assumptions are stated;
- 16-week Phase-2 engineering-model schedule is requirements-linked;
- every major risk has a test, fallback, and decision rule;
- no flight-readiness claim is made before qualification evidence exists.

- Government-funding eligibility statement confirmed

## Hard NO-GO conditions
- Fabricated or relabeled measured data
- Contradictory architecture definitions
- Unsupported rank/condition-number headline
- Claimed 300–800 km hardware performance without hardware evidence
- Cost target represented as quoted when it is only estimated
- Final submission made at the deadline without upload/rendering verification
