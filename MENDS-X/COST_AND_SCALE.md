# MENDS-X Cost and Scale Model

Status: engineering estimate pending dated supplier/facility quotes and measured labor/yield.

## 100-unit recurring delivered-cost planning cases

- Low case: **$4,280/unit**
- Base case: **$7,220/unit**
- High case: **$11,750/unit**
- Design target: **< $10,000/unit at quantity 100**

The base case leaves $2,780/unit of margin to the target; the high case misses by $1,750/unit. This identifies calibration, yield, and supplier pricing as major cost gates.

## Throughput planning model
Two parallel calibration fixtures, 6 hours per unit, 40 hours/week, and 90% first-pass yield produce approximately **12 accepted units/week**, above the 10/week production objective.

## Manufacturing concept
- Batch photochemical or laser-cut precision grids/electrodes
- Simple machined/formed metal structures
- Commercial ceramic/PEEK insulators
- Replaceable cathode/source cartridge
- Standardized guarded PCB electronics
- Shared vacuum manifold and scripted gas/electrical calibration
- Unit-specific coefficients retained digitally after acceptance

## Quote requirements before final submission
Obtain dated 10/100/500 quantity quotes for precision sensor metalwork, custom electronics, cathode/source module, enclosure/mount and relevant harnessing; obtain facility quotes for vacuum/gas calibration, atomic-oxygen work, and relevant environmental testing. Include calibration labor, acceptance yield, rework, environmental screening and overhead in delivered recurring cost.

## Economic scenario
NASA states comparable instruments are roughly $1M–$10M per copy and seeks solutions producible at quantities of at least 100. At a $10,000/unit recurring target, 100 MENDS-X sensors would cost $1M versus a $100M–$1B comparison range, a potential recurring acquisition-cost difference of approximately $99M–$999M under that explicit comparison. This is not a claim NASA would otherwise procure 100 legacy instruments.
