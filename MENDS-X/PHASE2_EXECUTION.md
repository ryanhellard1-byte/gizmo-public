# MENDS-X Phase 2 — 16-Week Execution Plan

NASA Phase 2 gives winners four months to finalize the design, build an engineering model, and demonstrate the required measurement in ground testing.

## Weeks 1–2
Freeze requirements, host assumptions, interfaces, parts, and facility bookings. Exit: ICD v0.1 and controlled BOM.

## Weeks 2–3
G0/G1 guarded dark-baseline and steering-only null testing. Exit: measured baseline/drift and steering-synchronous residual; redesign if the ≤0.5 fA allocation is missed.

## Weeks 3–5
G2 electron-on/gas-off map versus energy, field state, electron current, and temperature. Exit: measured background distribution/covariance.

## Weeks 5–7
G3 pure N2/O2/He 5E×2F response calibration and electron-energy metrology. Exit: measured response matrix, repeatability, linearity, energy centroid/EEDF width, covariance.

## Weeks 7–8
G4 blind mixtures with coefficients and estimator frozen before reveal. Internal goal: ≥95% of supported cases within ±15% absolute density error.

## Weeks 8–10
Detailed engineering-model mechanical/electrical design. Freeze CAD, power, thermal, interface, contamination, and EMI assumptions.

## Weeks 10–12
Build flight-like engineering model.

## Weeks 12–14
Functional verification plus thermal and EMI pre-compliance work; measure actual mass, power, data rate, and repeatability.

## Weeks 14–16
G6: propagate measured response/background/EEDF distributions through the unchanged 23,760-state NRLMSIS campaign. Freeze truthful altitude claim and prepare site-visit evidence.

## Atomic oxygen
G5 atomic-O response should be completed as early as facility access permits. If not available before Phase-1 submission, preserve a dated qualified-facility plan and close it during Phase 2.

## Current hosted-payload planning budget
- Mass: 270 g roll-up, <300 g target
- Volume: <0.5U target
- Average power: 4.5 W roll-up, <5 W target
- Peak power: 10 W planning ceiling; requires margin reduction/verification
- Normal telemetry: ~0.5–1.0 kbps
- Ram pointing: ±5° baseline target; characterize to at least ±10°
- No independent radio, propulsion, deployable boom, or released object

Host-dependent radiation, vibration/shock, thermal, and EMI/EMC requirements remain open until a flight provider is selected.
