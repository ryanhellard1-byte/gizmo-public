# Hellard Law candidate v0.1

## Status

This is a research hypothesis, not an established law of nature.

## Candidate principle

**Hellard Spectral-Dominance Law (candidate):**

A dynamical degree of freedom departs from Newtonian inertia only when its acceleration lies in the low-acceleration regime and its causal scale-localized motion is dynamically dominant over slower environmental motion. Faster internal motion is effaced from slower center-of-mass dynamics by a scale-free causal memory hierarchy.

## Isolated circular limit

Use

\[
\mu(x)=\frac{x}{\sqrt{1+x^2}},\qquad x=a/a_H,
\]

with empirical scale

\[
a_H\approx 1.1\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}.
\]

The isolated circular law is

\[
a\,\mu(a/a_H)=g_N.
\]

For \(a\ll a_H\),

\[
a\simeq\sqrt{a_H g_N},
\]

so

\[
v^4=GMa_H.
\]

## Causal scale-free memory

For every \(\lambda>0\), introduce a causal memory state

\[
\dot m_\lambda+\lambda m_\lambda=\lambda a(t).
\]

The measure \(d\ln\lambda\) is scale invariant.

The first log-scale band is

\[
q_\lambda=\partial_{\ln\lambda}m_\lambda.
\]

For a harmonic acceleration, it obeys

\[
2\int_0^\infty |q_\lambda|^2 d\ln\lambda=|A|^2.
\]

The higher-order effacement band is

\[
b_\lambda=(\partial_{\ln\lambda}^2-\partial_{\ln\lambda})m_\lambda,
\]

with normalized power kernel

\[
K_2(x)=\frac{4x^4}{(1+x^2)^3}.
\]

For a frequency hierarchy \(R\gg1\), fast-mode contamination of a slow band scales as

\[
K_2(1/R)\sim4R^{-4}.
\]

## Falsification rule

The candidate is rejected if a single universal parameter set cannot simultaneously satisfy galaxy rotation data, Solar-System dynamics, wide-binary data, conservation/stability requirements, and the eventual relativistic lensing/cosmology tests.
