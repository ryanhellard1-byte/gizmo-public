# Relativistic lensing obstruction for pure modified inertia

A nonrelativistic modified-inertia law by itself is not a complete alternative to dark matter/gravity.

Assume simultaneously that:

1. the Einstein/Poisson gravitational field equations are unchanged and sourced only by the observed baryons;
2. photons follow the standard null geodesics of that unchanged metric;
3. only the inertia/trajectories of massive nonrelativistic bodies are modified.

Then gravitational lensing remains the baryonic-GR lensing prediction. The additional dynamical acceleration inferred in galaxies does not automatically appear in photon deflection.

Therefore a Hellard theory intended to explain both galaxy dynamics and lensing must relax at least one of assumptions 1-3. A relativistic completion must modify the metric field equations, introduce additional relativistic fields/stress-energy, or modify how all matter including photons couples to the physical metric/effective geometry.

This is not optional. It is a structural requirement for any claim that the theory replaces dark matter rather than merely parametrizing nonrelativistic dynamics.

Current MOND literature makes the same broad point: nonrelativistic MOND is phenomenology until embedded in a relativistic theory; lensing and cluster residual missing mass remain major discriminants.
